Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This page explains what The Capital Code collects through this website (the Screener and calculators, Nexus AI, Codeflow and GreekWatch), why, and what you can ask us to do about it. We keep it short because we collect little.

What we collect, and when

  • Waitlists and invites (Nexus AI, Codeflow, GreekWatch). Your email, and where the form asks for them, your name, mobile number (Nexus only), an invite code if you have one, and any suggestion you type. We also create an invite code for you so you can share your place in line.
  • Contact form. Your name, email, the topic you pick and your message.
  • Technical data. Our host receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, as every web server does, and keeps standard logs.
  • On your own device. Your light or dark theme choice and screener items you save are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device.

What we do not collect

No bank logins, no card details, no PAN or Aadhaar, no precise location, and no advertising or tracking cookies. The Screener and calculators run in your browser; the numbers you type into a calculator are not sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To run the waitlists: save your place, apply invite-code priority, and tell you when your batch or a product opens.
  • To read and answer your messages, and to consider your suggestions when deciding what to build.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your data, rent it out or use it for third-party advertising. We will only email you about the product you joined and about replies to your messages.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores waitlist and contact records in a cloud database. Direct access to those tables is closed; the site writes to them only through restricted functions.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts may serve typefaces to the Nexus page, which means your browser contacts Google when that page loads.
  • When you tap a share or social link (WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook), you leave our site and that service's own policy applies.

How long we keep it

Waitlist records are kept while the waitlist or the product they relate to is active. Contact messages are kept for as long as needed to reply and follow up. You can ask us to delete your records at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to being emailed, whenever you like. Send the request through the Contact page and use the email address you joined with so we can find your record. This follows the rights given by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This site is for adults. Please do not give us a child's details.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we update this page and its date.

Questions about this page, or a request about your data? Use the Contact page and choose the topic that fits. We reply to every one.
