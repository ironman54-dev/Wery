A tour of the workspaces

One idea in every workspace.

Eleven tabs, and each one does something a normal terminal does not.

01

Spot · quant inspector

Every chart gets a second opinion.

Beside the candles sits a panel that audits the stock: an 8-point Stage-2 trend check, a volume footprint, an earnings guardrail and a position sized to your risk.

  • Minervini Stage-2 template, scored point by point
  • Position sized to 1R before you press Buy
  • Hide, reorder and resize every card

02

Breadth · open the math

Every verdict shows its working.

The market regime and the follow-through-day tracker are not black boxes. Tap "i" and read the exact rule, with today's live inputs beside it.

  • Regime verdict scored from live inputs
  • O'Neil follow-through day tracked step by step
  • Computed from daily bars of the stocks it tracks

03

Rotation radar

Watch money rotate between sectors.

A relative rotation graph with comet tails shows where each sector came from, not just where it sits. Press play and watch the rotation unfold.

  • Sectors, industries, indices or single stocks
  • Tails, quadrants and a replay slider
  • Click a node to drill into its stocks

04

Screener builder

Screens that can screen indicators.

Compare an indicator with another indicator: RSI against its own EMA, Supertrend on RSI, EMA on volume. Save the rule sets, or paste a formula.

  • 16 metrics with AND or OR logic
  • Study-on-study metrics inside your rules
  • Saved screeners and a quick formula parser

05

Systems · 4-phase exit engine

A stop that climbs with the trade.

Most backtests use one fixed stop. CodeFlow's exit is a state machine: it holds early, then ratchets the stop up as the trade proves itself.

  • Hard stop first, then breakeven plus 0.15R at +1.2R
  • Floor and trail: lock +1R, then trail with an ATR chandelier
  • Every rule written out before you run the test

06

AI strategy architect

Describe it. Get a testable system.

Write a strategy in plain English. CodeFlow compiles it into a step-by-step flow with entry, stop and exit phases, then loads it into the backtester.

  • Readable steps, not a black box
  • Same 4-phase exit engine underneath
  • One click to open it in Systems

07

Algos · your edge, simulated

250 futures, from your own trades.

Run 250 random trade sequences from your win rate and R-multiples, or load your realised journal. See the equity paths, the worst losing streak and Half-Kelly sizing.

  • Load your journal to calibrate it on your real edge
  • Risk of ruin and 95th percentile loss streak
  • Full and Half-Kelly to stop over-leveraging

08

Live desk

Spikes and concentration, before you look.

A 5-minute feed flags sudden surges and flushes across your watchlist. A sector donut warns when one sector passes 35% of your capital.

  • 2% to 5% thresholds on 5-minute bars
  • Sparklines and one-click chart inspect
  • Concentration alerts and portfolio heat

09

Journal

See which setups actually pay.

A heatmap crosses every setup with its R-multiple outcomes, then writes the verdict: where your right-tail winners come from and which setup to size down.

  • Setup by R-multiple matrix
  • Expectancy, profit factor and +2R share per setup
  • Calendar coloured by average R

10

Study

Learn a setup before you trade it.

Curated playbooks on real NSE charts, such as TRENT's volatility contraction and HAL's high tight flag. Each one has an entry checklist, invalidation rules and a replay drill.

  • Entry checklist and invalidation criteria
  • Replay drill with a Buy button and a score
  • Blind mode hides the answer

Questions

Before you join

Is this investment advice?

No. CodeFlow is a research and learning tool. It does not tell you what to buy or sell, and The Capital Code is not a SEBI-registered adviser.

What does "shows its working" mean?

Verdicts such as the market regime and the follow-through day come with an "i" button. It opens the exact rule and the live numbers that produced the answer, so you can check it yourself.

Can my AI assistant use it?

Yes. CodeFlow includes an MCP server with seven tools, including pattern scans, breadth, backtests and Monte Carlo. Orders through the tool are paper trades only.

Which markets does it cover?

NSE and BSE cash stocks, sector indices, and NIFTY and BANKNIFTY options. Everything is in rupees.

When do I get in?

CodeFlow is still in production and testing, so there is no date yet. When it is ready we open the doors in small batches, in the order people join, and email you when yours is ready.

Are these real screens?

The two screenshots are captures of the running app from 20 November 2026, in the Daylight theme. The animated panels are illustrations of how each feature works, with synthetic data.
