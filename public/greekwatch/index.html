The read

The side decaying faster is the side being written.

Writers want premium to fall. So when one side's Greeks decay faster than the other's since the open, that side is being sold into, and that points somewhere. GreekWatch measures it per strike, per side, all day.

  • ✓One formula, always. Each change is open minus current, summed across strikes inside a stated delta band. Nothing is a mystery score.
  • ✓Vega, theta and OI, weighted. 50, 30 and 20. When vega and theta disagree, it says No Trade instead of averaging them.
  • ✓Four indices cross-checked. A call counts for more when the other indices agree, and VIX is read as both a level and a trend since the open.

Trend

Watch it build, minute by minute.

Every minute of the session is recorded, so the day is a line you can read, not a number that flickers. Theta, vega, gamma and OI each get a chart against spot, with decay pressure underneath.

  • ✓Every chart says how to read it. The rule sits right under the chart, in plain words.
  • ✓Time Machine. Replay a past session and see exactly what the screen would have said at any minute.
  • ✓Export. Take the session out as a file when you want to study it elsewhere.

Time Machine

Rewind any session, one minute at a time.

Pick a recorded day and the whole app travels back to it. Scrub to 12:09, step a frame forward, or let it play at four or sixteen times speed and watch the read form.

  • ✓Exactly what you would have seen. The table, the verdict and every chart are rebuilt from the saved minutes, so a replay never rewrites history.
  • ✓Four indices, one playhead. Switch between NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, SENSEX and FINNIFTY while the clock stays where you left it.
  • ✓Good for a post-mortem. Check what the Greeks were doing before a move, not after it.

Questions

Before you open it

Is this a tip service?

No. GreekWatch shows a reading of market data using a stated method. It does not give buy or sell recommendations and places no orders. The Capital Code is not a SEBI-registered adviser, and options carry a high risk of loss.

Where does the data come from?

Live option prices and open interest come from the Kotak Neo API through the app's own server. The Greeks are then priced by GreekWatch itself from those prices.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login in the app. Open it and it works.

When does it update?

During market hours, 09:15 to 15:30 IST. Outside them you can replay recorded sessions in Time Machine.
