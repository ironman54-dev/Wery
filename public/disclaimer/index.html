Screener and calculators

Screener lists and backtests use historical NSE closing prices. They leave out taxes, brokerage, slippage and other costs, and past results do not guarantee future returns. Calculators give estimates from the numbers you enter and the assumptions shown; real returns will differ.

Options and GreekWatch

GreekWatch shows a reading of options data using a stated method. It is a study tool, not a trading signal, and it places no orders. Trading in options and other derivatives carries a high risk of loss, and most individual traders lose money. Never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

Nexus AI

Nexus shows your own entries and, for learning, paper-trading market signals. Screens shown on the site use sample data. Answers from the assistant should be checked against your own records before you act on them.

Codeflow

Codeflow is still in production and testing. Some data inside it is sample data until a live feed is connected. Nothing it shows is a recommendation.
