How it works

Three steps to a clear picture.

You do not have to type your whole life in on day one.

1

Add what you have

Fill one Excel once, or add holdings by hand. Accounts, cards, FDs, funds, shares, loans, income, reminders and goals all have a place.

2

Approve, don't type

Entries arrive as cards and nothing is saved until you say yes. Anything over the limit you set waits for your approval.

3

Ask, and decide

See the net worth, the calendar and the signals, or just ask in plain words and drag the numbers to see what changes.

Wealth · net worth

A net worth you can actually trust.

Enter each holding once. Wealth keeps the total, fills the calendar with every EMI, SIP, premium and maturity, and shows one short list of what needs you today.

  • ✓Mark paid from the alarm. Dues ring the evening before and the morning of, with a Mark paid button.

Spends · every rupee

Every rupee, in one honest book.

You log your own spends, your budget burns down live, and anything over a limit you set waits for your yes. One month total, the same on every screen. Link a family and the shared totals show up for everyone in it.

  • ✓Bank SMS become entries you approve. Read on the phone; the message never leaves it.

Markets · market signals

See the setup, not just the signal.

Pick the strategies you want to follow. Every evening Nexus checks the day's NSE files and lists the stocks that meet their rules. Tap one and the chart shows why.

  • ✓The strategy drawn on the chart. Its EMAs, RSI and levels on real candles, the signal candle marked, Day, Week or Month.
  • ✓The rules checked again. Each rule with the actual numbers on the signal day.
  • ✓Your own marks. Add or hide indicators, draw Fib levels, channels and boxes; they are saved per stock.
  • ✓Data that is checked, not guessed. Each file is cross-checked against NSE's other files before a day is published, and splits apply only when the price gap confirms them.

Privacy

A vault, not a data pipe.

The less we hold, the less anyone can lose. Here is what stays on your phone and what syncs.

SMS stay on the phone

Bank messages are read by the on-device reader. They are not uploaded and not sent to any AI.

Private by default

Every record belongs to one person, and database rules let only that person read it.

Family sees only what you share

Mark something shared and your linked family can see it. They cannot change it.

Masked before any cloud AI

Names, account numbers and FD numbers are masked on the phone before a cloud AI call.

No bank connection

Nexus never logs in to your bank. Everything comes from what you enter, approve or receive by SMS.

Sync that cannot wipe you

Deletes sync as markers, so an empty cloud can never erase what is on your phone.

Questions

Before you join

Does Nexus connect to my bank account?

No. It reads the bank SMS on your phone and what you enter or approve. There is no bank login, no account aggregator and no scraping.

Who can see my money?

Only you. If you link a family, only the items you mark as shared show, as totals. Ledgers and line items stay yours.

How does the family link work?

Every person has their own Nexus account. One person creates a family and gets a unique family code; the others enter it on their own phone to join. You can leave a family whenever you like and your own data stays yours.

Are the market signals investment advice?

No. It shows signals from strategies you choose on end-of-day NSE data, for paper trading and learning. Nexus is not a SEBI-registered adviser and places no orders.

Does it work without the internet?

Mostly. Your data, the AI and five years of market history live on the phone. New market days and family sync arrive when you are online.

Which phones?

Android first. The web version runs in any modern browser.

What does it cost?

Free for everyone in the beta batches.
