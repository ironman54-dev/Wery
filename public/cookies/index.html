Cookies and Storage

Last updated 24 November 2026

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run analytics that follow you around the web.

What is stored on your device

  • Theme choice. Whether you picked the light or the dark look, so the site opens the way you left it.
  • Saved screener items. Anything you save in the Screener stays in your browser only.

This is kept in your browser's local storage, not sent to us. You can clear it any time from your browser's site settings, and the site will keep working.

Our host and fonts

Our host, Vercel, and the font service used by the Nexus page (Google Fonts) receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads, as part of normal web delivery. See the Privacy Policy for more.
